Requirements Checklist

  • At least 12 characters
  • Contains lowercase letters (a-z)
  • Contains uppercase letters (A-Z)
  • Contains numbers (0-9)
  • Contains special characters (!@#$%^&*)
  • No common sequences (123, abc, qwerty)
  • No repeated characters (aaa, 111)

Password Best Practices

Use a Passphrase

Combine 4-6 random words: correct-horse-battery-staple is stronger than Tr0ub4dor&3

Unique Per Account

Never reuse passwords. Use a password manager to generate and store unique passwords for each service.

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds a critical second layer of security beyond just your password.

Check for Breaches

Use services like Have I Been Pwned to check if your passwords have appeared in data breaches.
